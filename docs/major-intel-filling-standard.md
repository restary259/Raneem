# Major Intelligence — filling standard

Rules for every team member who fills a subject card in the internal majors tool.

**The one rule everything else follows from:** a line on a card is either backed by an
official document, or it stays visibly empty. An empty line is a correct answer. A
guessed line is a mistake, even when it turns out to be right.

---

## 1. Evidence

- Every fact needs a source: the exact page or PDF, never a homepage.
- Every fact needs the date it was checked and the name of the person who checked it.
- Allowed sources, in this order of authority:
  1. The university's own programme page and its admission regulations
     (Zulassungsordnung / Studien- und Prüfungsordnung).
  2. anabin, KMK, hochschulstart, uni-assist.
  3. The official test provider: TestDaF, telc, DSH, TestAS, Goethe.
  4. The partner school's own current price list or student-information PDF.
- Not allowed as a source: agency blogs, forums, YouTube, social media, ChatGPT or any
  AI output, another agency's website, our own older documents, and memory.
- If two official sources disagree, record both and mark the line as a conflict. Never
  pick one and never average them.

## 2. No assumptions

- A rule that is not published is written as: *no official requirement found — do not
  assume one.*
- Never copy a requirement from a similar university, a similar subject, or an earlier
  year.
- No rounding, no "roughly", no "about".
- Do not reword a requirement. Use the university's own wording, then the translation
  next to it.

## 3. Highest requirement only

- Record the requirement that actually gets a seat, not the bare minimum needed to press
  submit. For a restricted programme, the competitive level is the fact; the
  "minimum to apply" line is never recorded on its own.
- If an extra step exists — TestAS, aptitude test, interview, Studienkolleg, portfolio,
  motivation letter — record it as required, with its own source.
- Silence is not proof. A page that does not mention TestAS is not evidence that TestAS
  is not required; check the admission regulations before writing anything.
- Language: record the level the university states, together with the certificates it
  names, in its own wording.

## 4. Prices and partner schools

- Course and accommodation prices come only from the current official price list or
  student-information PDF of a school we work with, for the correct year.
- Record separately: the price, the currency, what it includes, what it excludes, the
  deposit, and the arranging fee.
- A price from a previous year is either labelled clearly as an estimate based on that
  year, or left out.
- Never quote a price to a student from a card line whose check date is out of date.

## 5. Calculated values

- The Bagrut → German grade result is a calculation, never an admission answer, and is
  always shown with that label.
- A calculated value can never satisfy an official requirement.

## 6. Verification process

1. **Fill** — the team member enters the facts, each with its source link, check date and
   their own name.
2. **Review** — a second person opens every source link and confirms each value word for
   word.
3. **Mark verified** — only when every line either carries a source or is explicitly
   marked as not published.
4. **Re-check** — deadlines and admission mode every 4 months; everything else once a
   year. A card past its date is shown as out of date, not as verified.
5. **Fail** — any line that fails a re-check goes back to "not filled in yet". It is
   never left on screen while in doubt.

## 7. Clarity

- One fact per line, in English and Arabic.
- Western numerals (0–9) and full dates (DD.MM.YYYY).
- No marketing wording, no promises, no "usually", no "most students".
- Write what the source says. Nothing more.
