## Facts used (from the official 2026 programme price list already stored in the system)

- Intensive 20: 20 teaching hours/week, 09:00–12:15 and 13:00–14:30, ages 16+, levels A1–C2. Absolute beginners start on the published beginner dates; students with prior German may start any Monday.
- Course price per week by booking length: 1–6 weeks €225, 7–16 weeks €195, 17–29 weeks €170, 30+ weeks €155.
- Level durations: A1, A2, B1, C1 each 8–11 weeks; B2 12–15 weeks. A1→C1 is therefore roughly 44–59 weeks — presented as a planning range, not as a school promise.
- Host family, category A (up to 30 min to school): single room only €245/wk, with breakfast €280, half board €315. Twin room prices are per room.
- Host family, category B (up to 50 min): single room only €220/wk, breakfast €235, half board €275.
- Residences: category A single €155/wk (shower/WC in hallway, shared kitchen, 1.5–2.5 km from school), category B single €180/wk, category B twin €245/wk.
- Deposit €200 (€500 for stays of 12 weeks or more). Administration fee €130 (€170 from 12 weeks).
- Included with the course: placement test, level tests, certificate of participation; internal exam from €80, internationally recognised exams from €180.

Anything the official price list does not print is left out or written as "to be confirmed with the school" — no invented numbers, no invented travel times. Where a room is shared, the guide states plainly that a private flat is not guaranteed at short notice and a kitchen or bathroom may be shared.

## Money presentation

School costs in €, the DARB office fee in ₪, in separate blocks. Deposits and refundable amounts are shown outside the total. Arabic text runs right-to-left with prices, Latin names and URLs kept in correct left-to-right order.

## Photos

Official F+U and Heidelberg imagery sourced from the school's own pages and public city sources, with housing photos matched to the housing type described. Where no reliable official photo exists for a specific room type, the section runs without a photo rather than using a misleading stock image.

## Quality check

Every page is rendered to an image and inspected for Arabic letter shaping, RTL order, price direction, clipped text, spacing and photo placement before the file is delivered.
