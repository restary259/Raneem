# Deep dimensional color cards

## Direction
Replace the flat pastel treatment with the selected **Deep Dimensional Ruby** look: saturated, darker color, a directional light edge, recessed icon area, and a raised lower action area. Keep DARB's existing typography, real card copy, and readable contrast. Adapt the ruby treatment across subject categories so the grid has distinct, coordinated deep colors rather than becoming one solid red field.

## Scope
- Restyle the study-program cards with the selected composition: icon and category in the top row, title and description beneath, and a tactile “read more” area at the bottom. Keep the entire card as one accessible click target that opens the existing details; do not nest a button inside it.
- Give the six homepage choice cards the same dimensional finish in coordinated colors, without changing their destinations or the Apply button's priority.
- Keep forms, booking, article content, and signed-in dashboards unchanged.

## Technical details
- Replace the previous soft card-surface tokens with deep category surface pairs and legible foreground/highlight tokens in the global design system; use semantic classes in the two card components.
- Create depth through restrained CSS gradients, top-edge highlights, inset and outer shadows, and a small hover/focus lift, rather than a 3D engine or blurred decorative orbs. Honor reduced-motion preferences.
- Check Arabic RTL and English, phone and desktop layouts, card opening/navigation, contrast, and the preview build.
