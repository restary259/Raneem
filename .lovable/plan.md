## E. Monthly accommodation breakdown

HORIZONTE charges **by week (Sunday–Saturday)**, not by month, and the first four weeks are a block price that already contains a one-time fee. So the monthly view is presented as a planning aid only, headed **"تقسيم تقديري للتكلفة حسب الشهر لأغراض التخطيط"**, built like this:

- Weeks 1–4 (14.03 – 10.04.2027) shown as one block at the official 4-week price.
- From week 5 on, each further week at the official weekly rate (€100 / €110 / €150 / €170 / €220 by option).
- Each month lists the actual accommodation weeks that fall inside it and multiplies by that rate — never a total divided by 8.
- The €240 summer surcharge appears only in July and August, in the weeks it actually applies.
- A closing line: the sum of the months equals the official total; HORIZONTE invoices per booking, not monthly.
