# About DARB page rebuild

## Direction
Rebuild `/about` in the selected **Grounded narrative storyteller** direction: calm, editorial, human, and credible. It will share the visual language of Home, Destinations, and Services without copying their layouts.

The page will avoid emotional founder mythology, generic corporate claims, oversized value cards, and unsupported facts. Copy will be concise and natural in English, Arabic, and Hebrew.

## Page structure
1. **Shared About hero**
   - Keep the established DARB photo-banner treatment and consistent hero width.
   - Use a short title and one grounded supporting sentence.
   - No hero button; the homepage remains the only primary action hero.

2. **Our story**
   - Replace the current alternating card timeline with a quieter editorial narrative.
   - Explain the practical reason DARB exists: students need one clear path through information, preparation, documents, and decisions.
   - Use a path-line motif and three undated chapters rather than invented founding dates or milestones.

3. **Mission and vision**
   - Present these as two concise, contrasting statements rather than marketing cards.
   - Mission: make each next step understandable and organized.
   - Vision: help students make informed international-study decisions with clarity and responsibility.

4. **How we work**
   - Show grounded operating commitments such as listening before advising, explaining responsibilities, organizing the process, and staying reachable.
   - Tie each commitment to observable behavior; avoid claims like “best,” “prestigious,” or “exceeding expectations.”

5. **The people behind DARB**
   - Prepare a visually intentional team section for future real portraits, role titles, direct emails, and responsibility summaries.
   - Do not display invented staff, stock portraits, placeholder identities, or fake biographies now.
   - Until verified profiles are supplied, render only a short team introduction or omit the roster entirely so the page never looks unfinished.

6. **Practical closing**
   - End with the existing restrained contact area, not another sales-heavy banner.
   - Keep contact details and actions consistent with the rest of the public site.

## Visual treatment
- Use the existing DARB semantic colors and typography; do not import a new visual system.
- Favor editorial spacing, thin rules, asymmetrical text/image composition, small numbered markers, and alternating background bands.
- Avoid nested cards, decorative blobs, large icon grids, fake quotes, and excessive animation.
- Add only subtle entrance and image motion, with reduced-motion support.
- Keep layouts stable in LTR and RTL, with logical alignment and no fixed heights that could clip translations.

## Content and truthfulness cleanup
- Remove the current invented CEO identity, fake team profiles, stock headshots, unverified office/experience statements, and unsupported university-partnership language from the About experience.
- Rewrite the existing emotional or over-promotional story, values, and feature copy into specific, down-to-earth language.
- Preserve the existing public restriction against named language-school claims.
- Add complete English, Arabic, and Hebrew About-page copy with matching meaning, natural phrasing, and no terminal punctuation in titles.

## Technical scope
- Refactor the About page into small, focused sections following the existing public-page patterns.
- Keep the shared header, footer, hero component, contact component, semantic design tokens, and existing navigation behavior.
- Add route-specific About metadata if the route does not already provide it.
- Remove or retire obsolete About-only components once confirmed unused; do not change unrelated public pages.
