# Major Intelligence — filling standard for the team

A written rulebook the team must follow when filling a subject card. Zero assumptions: a card line is either backed by an official document, or it stays visibly empty.

## What gets created

1. **The standard, in English and Arabic** — two documents, same content, kept with the project so they can be updated as rules change.
2. **A printable PDF** of the Arabic version for the team to keep open while working.
3. **A short "Rules" link inside the majors page** opening a compact summary of the same rules, so nobody fills a card without seeing them.

## The rules the documents will state

### 1. Evidence
- Every fact needs a source: the exact page or PDF, not a homepage, plus the date it was checked and who checked it.
- Allowed sources, in this order: the university's own programme page and admission regulations; anabin / KMK / hochschulstart / uni-assist; the official test provider (TestDaF, telc, DSH, TestAS); the partner school's own current price list or student-information PDF.
- Not allowed as a source: agency blogs, forums, YouTube, ChatGPT, another agency's website, our own older documents, or memory.
- Two official sources disagreeing is recorded as a conflict showing both, never resolved by choosing one.

### 2. No assumptions
- A rule that is not published is written as "no official requirement found — do not assume one".
- Nothing is copied from a similar university, a similar subject, or a previous year.
- No rounding, no "roughly", no translating a requirement into a different wording than the university uses.

### 3. Highest requirement only
- Record the requirement that actually gets a seat, not the bare minimum to apply. Where a programme is restricted, the competitive level is the fact to record; the "minimum to submit" line is not recorded on its own.
- If an extra step exists — TestAS, aptitude test, interview, Studienkolleg, portfolio — it is recorded as required, with its own source. Silence about a step is not proof the step does not exist.
- Language: record the level the university states, together with the certificates it names in its own wording.

### 4. Prices and partner schools
- Course and accommodation prices come only from the current official price list or student-information PDF of the school we work with, for the correct year.
- Record the price, the currency, what it covers, what it excludes, the deposit, and the arranging fee separately.
- Prices from a previous year are marked as an estimate and labelled as such, or left out.
- Nothing is quoted to a student from a card line without the source date being current.

### 5. Calculated values
- The Bagrut → German grade result is a calculation, never an admission answer, and is always shown with that label.
- A calculation can never be used to satisfy an official requirement.

### 6. Verification process
- Filling: the team member enters facts with source link, check date and their name.
- Review: a second person opens each source link and confirms the value word for word before the subject is marked verified.
- A subject is only marked verified when every line either has a source or is explicitly marked as not published.
- Re-check schedule: deadlines and admission mode every 4 months; everything else once a year; a card past its date is shown as out of date, not as verified.
- Any line that fails re-check goes back to "not filled in yet" instead of staying on screen.

### 7. Clarity
- One fact per line, in the university's own terms, in English and Arabic.
- Western numerals and full dates.
- No marketing wording, no promises, no "usually" or "most students".
