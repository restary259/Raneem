# DARB intent-based visitor funnel

## Goal
Make the public website feel like a capable, real organization that understands Arab 48 students: “Tell us what you want; we’ll help figure out the rest.” This rollout covers the **website only**, not new social posts or campaigns. Keep the existing **four-step application** and the existing case/referral pipeline.

## What changes

1. **Visitor journey and office presence.** Refocus the home, services, about, contact, and office/location pages around four questions: Is this for me? Can DARB help in my situation? What happens next? How do I start? Use professional, natural Arabic and matching English, retain the established polished, large-organization visual identity, and make the Tamra office and in-person consultation/document handling visible as part of the service experience. Keep service details accessible without making internal packages the main visitor journey. Do not invent an office, address, hours, outcome, or guaranteed response time.
2. **Four easy application steps.** Keep the four-step structure and the shared public/partner form. Ask first for identity/contact, broad education context, what the student wants, and whether anyone is coming with them. Reveal only relevant follow-ups. For a companion, collect just identifying/contact information at this stage; leave their academic details for later, and clearly indicate when an individual companion could not be registered. Preserve required privacy consent, optional marketing consent, duplicate protection, and server-side source/referral attribution. Keep referral verification/status internal; a missing/invalid code must never alarm a student or block a normal application.
3. **Clear completion screen.** After successful submission, confirm receipt, explain that DARB reviews the request and will contact the student, and identify an office appointment as the likely next milestone. Avoid a precise time promise unless it is operationally verified. Offer two equally clear choices: **Book an office visit now** and **Let DARB contact me**. Submission succeeds independently of booking.
4. **Optional self-booking and later changes.** Present real available office slots, let the applicant reserve one against the case just created, show a confirmation, and give them a safe way to reschedule or cancel later. Keep the booking associated with the original case and visible in the existing staff appointment workflow. Do not expose staff calendars, student details, or arbitrary case identifiers publicly. Enforce availability, office schedule, idempotent booking, and collision prevention on the server, not just in the calendar UI. Use an opaque, expiring booking-management link or equivalent verified ownership check; never treat knowledge of a case ID or phone number alone as authority. Reuse existing appointment status and notification/reminder handling and ensure changes cancel stale reminders.
5. **Verification.** Test Arabic RTL and English, desktop and phone; four-step conditional paths, companion and no-companion, referrals (valid/invalid/unavailable), duplicate submits, booking versus deferring, concurrent slot claims, confirmation, reschedule/cancel, unauthorized link access, and case/staff-calendar linkage. Confirm no duplicate appointment or misleading success state when any step fails.

## Technical notes
- Current public application already has four steps and sends `ref_code` with `source: "apply_page"` to its case-creation handler; its last step currently asks for each companion’s education/passport/city/major and its success state offers only a website link. Its missing-referral toast and invalid-referral warning currently expose internal attribution language; remove the student-facing warnings without changing server attribution.
- Existing appointment records include `case_id`, staff assignment, schedule, and status, and the calendar picker is currently a team-side control. A new public booking path must return availability only, then perform booking and changes through guarded server-side operations with an atomic overlap check. Resolve staff/office allocation and booking hours from verified operational configuration before exposing slots; if that configuration is unavailable, show the “Let DARB contact me” path rather than fictional times.
- The application handler already responds with `case_id`; the public booking handoff must use an unguessable, short-lived authorization mechanism rather than putting a bare case ID in a public management URL. Keep existing consent and case-stage rules intact. New customer-facing wording belongs in both Arabic and English dictionaries.
- The uploaded companion/referral note duplicates the same requirements in both files: identify accompanying friends/family without a second long application; preserve accurate attribution silently.
